# LockedIN visual direction

Source: six images supplied by the user in chat on 2026-09-27, visually inspected, plus the recovered local mockup set. [Saved reference inventory](references/README.md). Interpretation below is a working reading of the references, not a claim of user approval of a new design.

## What the images communicate

### Welcome / room becoming a world (chat images 1 and 4)

A warm ivory canvas, strong dark title, amber primary action, and detailed isometric artwork dominate. The communal wooden table and students explain togetherness. The arrow to a floating district explains that effort builds a persistent place. Carry that connection into real progression, rather than adding an unrelated dashboard. Books, windows, foliage, wood texture and warm light create the inviting atmosphere.

### Avatar selection (image 2)

Eight distinct, full-body characters have readable faces, layered hair, clothing folds, separate shoes and grounded shadows. Their silhouettes and palettes are recognisable at phone scale. Selection is obvious, and the promise is that the chosen base can be customised next. This establishes a substantially richer target than a geometric placeholder or colour-only reskin.

### Results (image 3)

A celebratory medal leads into clear results, character portraits and a tangible world contribution. The room miniature and progress bar explain what studying achieved. Retain the emotional sequence, but use real values; negative rankings must respect opt-in competitive mode and be absent from supportive public results. Reference numbers are not production data.

### Classic Habbo customiser (image 5)

The important interaction is independent control of hair, face/skin, top, trousers and shoes with an immediately updated full-body preview. Preserve the freedom, recognisable proportions and readable pixel silhouette. The old desktop window, gender binary and tiny arrow targets are not requirements for the iPhone interface. The newer character-selection art supplies the richer detail target.

### Dark Room + World (image 6)

The scene occupies the largest portion of the screen. Students sit at properly grounded desks with coherent depth and occlusion. Amber lamplight, dark wood, books, rugs, plants and a night window make a quiet shared study space. Small labels distinguish focus, break and distraction without exposing private app activity. The district and building contributions connect individual sessions to collective progress.

## Combined direction

- Cream/amber onboarding and everyday shell, with a cosy evening room atmosphere. A dark live shell is a reference possibility, not a newly mandated global dark mode.
- Detailed pixel illustrations with intentional outlines, layered shading, coherent light and isometric perspective.
- Expressive small people with real hairstyles, clothes, shoes, standing/seated poses and consistent proportions.
- Rooms should feel furnished and inhabited; adding decorative noise is not a substitute for designed composition.
- Large, readable world/character art supported by restrained, usable controls. Use pixel display typography selectively; body text and controls remain legible.
- Persistent visual changes should result from actual earned progress. Avatar editing and furniture selection must change real scene layers.
- Keep status labels legible and use text/icons as well as colour. Quiet study is the core activity.

## Implementation quality gates

1. Render one personal room, one four-seat room and one avatar family in the actual app.
2. Show distinct hair, skin, tops, bottoms and shoes at normal phone size.
3. Verify seated characters, chairs and desks overlap correctly.
4. Verify furniture selection changes the room and persists.
5. Capture app screenshots and compare them with these references before declaring visual completion.
6. Demonstrate a completed session producing an earned visible improvement.

A compiled screen or generated flat mockup does not establish these gates. Existing procedural artwork is implementation material to assess, not the accepted target.
