# LockedIN interface decisions

Implemented direction, 2026-10-03. Based on the saved original mockups and the user's rejection of the basic character/UI pass. This is a design decision record, not a claim that the user approved final fidelity.

## Identity

The pixel people and rooms carry the personality. Keep real, independently editable sprites and furniture layers. Use warm ivory panels, restrained tan outlines and subtle lower-edge depth around them. Amber identifies the primary action; green indicates supportive focus. Modern rounded titles and readable body text support the artwork; small monospaced captions are accents, not the main reading face.

## Journey

- Home: identity and personal space first, then clear wardrobe/decorating routes, a labelled demo club and actual progression. The pinned primary action changes truthfully for active session, check-in and unread results.
- Setup: a compact character/club preview, a subject and achievable goal, then duration. Show the real seat count and honest simulation boundaries before starting.
- Focus: compact framed timer with actual subject/goal; room directly below; small status icons keep faces visible; names and detailed goals have a separate roster. Break/resume and interruption report stay reachable. Larger clubs retain two explicit seating areas.
- Check-in: private and supportive. The answer does not change the earned reward.
- Results: emotional completion first, then real recorded results and a tangible room purchase. Early exits receive no completion award. Measurement explanation can expand without dominating the first view.
- Wardrobe: keep the full outfit visible while browsing; show feature-specific sprite crops for hair/clothes/shoes. Distinguish wearing, owned and priced items. Save applies the outfit; purchased unlocks remain owned.
- Room studio: keep the actual room visible while browsing actual catalogue sprites/material previews. Placement saves immediately. Purchase feedback stays beside the bottom action, including insufficient coins.
- World: explain where the next completed session contributes. Locked plots explain the unlock path; insufficient coins produces feedback.

## Interaction rules

Use the shared Studio controls instead of per-screen variants. Important actions use at least44pt tap targets. Selected states use outlines/checkmarks/text, not just colour. Keep disabled actions visibly disabled. Preserve route and control accessibility identifiers for meaningful UI verification.

Do not copy chat controls, invite buttons, notifications, fake friends or mock numbers out of reference images without their functionality. Current companions remain simulated. Actual app blocking, multiplayer and shared server progression are separate milestones.

## Acceptance and remaining work

Inspect characters at normal phone size, including dark skin, glasses, long hair, independent jacket/skirt/boots and seated desk overlap. Walk the complete saved study-to-earned-item path. Source export and successful compilation alone are insufficient.

The current48×64 family has improved proportions and silhouettes; the original reference art still has richer hair/clothing volume and room atmosphere. Comprehensive Dynamic Type, physical-device accessibility and a full onboarding visual pass remain separate acceptance work. Never label simulator checks as physical-device verification.
